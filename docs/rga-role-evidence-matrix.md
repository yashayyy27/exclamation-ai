## Evidence boundaries

All public metrics are synthetic; no raw Google data, private customer records, actual model results, client adoption or outcomes are claimed. The public analyst is deterministic. Model choice is tested with mocks until an authenticated measured run exists. A small author-written release set has been inspected in deterministic testing and is not an independent generalization benchmark. Attribution examples are a separate source, not estimated real performance.

For the wider role brief: Python/SQL is evidenced by transformations and reconciliation tests; tracking QA by contracts and explicit missing external receipts; measurement frameworks by KPI → hypothesis → experiment mapping; automation by CI and scheduled fixture evaluation; stakeholder handover by runbooks, client exports and next-iteration PRD. Collaboration evidence is limited to documentation and an AI-assisted owner build. No team/client history or personal mastery is invented.

Read [verification](verification.md) for exact check counts and publication status, [audit](audit/2026-10-22-gap-report.md) for the prioritised baseline gaps, and [applicant contribution statement](demo-script.md) before presenting this work. PR #1 was merged and its Pages deployment succeeded; the live URLs include that recruitment upgrade. Publication and real integration verification are separate: no authenticated BigQuery, GA4/GTM or OpenAI receipt is claimed by the deployment.
